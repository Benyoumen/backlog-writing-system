BA-LEAD Leads become qualified conversations

EpicBA-LEAD · Leads become qualified conversations

A lead from a property portal reaches the broker with context and a drafted first reply, instead of a bare name and phone number.

3 Features · 10 User Stories
1Why it exists

A brokerage receives hundreds of portal leads a month and most die in the first hour: the broker is at a visit, the reply comes late, and the buyer has already written to three other brokers. Without this Epic the assistant has nothing to work with: no queue, no context, no first reply.

2Problem

Portal leads arrive by email and in three portal back offices. Brokers copy them by hand into the CRM, reply from memory, and lose the ones that arrive during visits.

3Objective

Every lead is in the broker's queue within a minute, with the listing, the buyer's message and the questions the broker still has to ask, and the first reply goes out the same hour after the broker approves it.

4Success metrics
  • First reply within one hour, from 35% to 80% of leads (illustrative).
  • Leads with a qualification score before the first visit: 70%.
  • Portal leads lost to a late reply, read from the portal's own report: half of today's number.
5First-release scope
  • The queue with context (BA-LEAD-F01).
  • The drafted first reply, sent after approval (BA-LEAD-F02).
  • Qualification questions and a score (BA-LEAD-F03).
6Business rules
  • The assistant never contacts a buyer without the broker's approval.
  • A lead belongs to one broker; redistribution is the manager's decision, not the assistant's.
  • The portal of origin and its lead id stay on the record.
7Dependencies

Portal exports (email and CSV) in wave 1; portal APIs are a later enabler. BA-CRM-F01 for any write to the CRM.

8Risks and mitigation
  • Risk: a portal changes its email format and leads stop arriving. Mitigation: a daily count per portal with an alert when it drops to zero.
  • Risk: the drafted reply sounds generic. Mitigation: the broker edits before approving, and edits feed the next drafts.
  • Risk: duplicate leads across portals. Mitigation: match by phone and email before creating a record.
9Governance and operation
  • The sales manager owns the queue rules and the redistribution.
  • Weekly reading of first-reply time per broker in the manager's digest.
  • Autonomy: the assistant drafts; sending stays at tier 2 (approve each) in the first release.
10Technical details
  • Inbound: portal emails parsed into a lead record; CSV import as a fallback.
  • Listing data from the brokerage's catalogue, read only.
  • Reads and writes go through the assistant's single CRM write path; every write carries author, previous value, proposed value, written value, origin and approval.

3 Features · 9 User Stories
1Why it exists

A visit is the most expensive moment of the sale and today it starts from memory and ends in a WhatsApp message that nobody records. Without this Epic the CRM shows visits that happened with no outcome, and the forecast reads a pipeline that does not exist.

3Objective
