# ACME-E01 - Customer follow-up is sent and logged within a day of every meeting

Type: Epic. Children: ACME-E01-F01 Meetings and notes, ACME-E01-F02 Follow-up draft and send, ACME-E01-F03 CRM write-back, ACME-E01-F04 Activity trail.

All values below are illustrative, for a fictional company.

## 1. Why it exists
After a customer meeting, Acme reps write the follow-up email and update the CRM days later, or not at all. Customers wait for an answer, and sales leaders forecast from records that no longer match the deals.

## 2. Problem
Reps meet several customers a day and have no time between visits. The follow-up email is written in the evening or skipped. CRM updates depend on the rep opening each opportunity and editing fields one by one. The sales director reviews the forecast every week against records that are often older than the last meeting.

## 3. Objective
Every customer meeting leads to a sent follow-up and an up-to-date CRM record within one working day, with the rep approving each action.

## 4. Success metrics
| Metric | Baseline | Target | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meetings followed up within one working day | To measure in the first two weeks | 80% | Per month |
| Opportunities with a CRM update after the last meeting | To measure in the first two weeks | 90% | Per month |
| Drafts the rep sends with no edits or small edits | Not applicable | 60% | First 8 weeks |

## 5. First-release scope
In: follow-up email drafts from meeting notes, rep approval, and CRM updates after approval.
Out: automatic sending without approval, meetings held by phone, languages other than English, and quote generation.

## 6. Business rules
- The agent never sends an email or writes to the CRM without the rep's explicit approval.
- The agent only reads and writes opportunities owned by the rep who held the meeting.
- A follow-up draft uses only facts from the rep's own meeting notes and the CRM record.

## 7. Dependencies
- Access to the calendar, to find meetings that ended.
- Access to the CRM, to read opportunities and write approved updates.
- A decision by the sales director on which CRM fields the agent may update (taken on 2026-03-02).

## 8. Risks and mitigation
| Risk | Owner | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Reps stop trusting the drafts after a wrong detail | Product manager | Show the source of each fact in the draft, and track the edit rate weekly |
| The agent writes a wrong value to the CRM | Sales operations | Write only approved fields, only after approval, and log every change with an undo option |

## 9. Governance and operation
The sales director approves the list of fields the agent may update. Sales operations monitors the weekly edit rate and the rejected updates. Changes to the field list follow the same approval.

## 10. Technical details
The agent reads meeting notes from the notes tool and opportunities through the CRM API. All CRM writes go through one write service. Drafts are generated by a language model, and every generation logs input, output and model version.
